Combinatorial background (CB) & Pairs of an $e^+$ and an $e^-$ that do not come from the same decay are removed by subtracting the like-sign pairs. & $\mathrm{CB}=2\sqrt{N_{++}N_{--}}$ from the $e^+e^+$ and $e^-e^-$ rows of the \emph{data} in 10\,\MeVcc \Mfour cells ($k=1$), linearly interpolated to 2.5\,\MeVcc and spread over the cells of a layout by the like-sign share of the 50\,\MeVcc band; error from 200 Poisson event-group replicas. The simulation never provides it. & yes (data) & CB$/N$: S0 B 0.056, P 0.059; ``pre'' B 0.101, P 0.103 \\
Luminosity and trigger word ($64/63$) & Turns counted rows into cross sections. & $\sigma=(N-\mathrm{CB})\times\frac{64}{63}/L$, $L=6079.2\,\nbinv$: elastic luminosity of the 17\,557 analysed runs (START-free chain, LH$_2$ only, sector-2 PT2 deficit removed inside $L$). $64/63$: 1/64 of the PT3 events carry the trigger word 4096, FAT keeps 8192. & yes & $\times1.01587/L$ ($\pm6.9$\,\%); old tables (6080.5, no $64/63$) $\times1.0161$ \\
Live time of the PT3 input & The PT3 live fraction may be lower than the PT2 one contained in $L$. & LF3/LF2 $\times64/63$ per day from the luminosity files (0.900--0.948, $L$-weighted 0.932--0.933). Unknown whether it applies to single-interaction PT3 events. & no & one-sided $+7.1$ to $+7.3$\,\% on cross sections \\
Mass scale of the simulation (D8) & The simulated $\eta$ peak sits at 0.5460\,\GeVcc, the data peak at 0.5439; the simulated \Mfour is moved onto the data scale. & $M_4'=s\,[m_4^{\mathrm{true}}+r_{\mathrm{res}}(m_4-m_4^{\mathrm{true}})]$, $s=0.99615$, $r_{\mathrm{res}}=1$ (rows without a true mass: $s\,m_4$); \Mpipi$\times0.9952$ from the K$^0_S$ position; \Mee not scaled. Variants $s=0.99521$, $0.99615\pm0.0009$, $r_{\mathrm{res}}=0.89/1.10$. & yes (sim) & ``pre'' data/sim against unscaled: B $-0.96$\,\%, P $-1.73$\,\%, $J$ $-2.5$\,\%; C5 in P at $r_{\mathrm{res}}=0.89/1.10$: $\times1.043/\times0.960$ \\
$\eta$ decay physics weights V3 (code 108) & The generator decays $\eta\to\ppee$, the $\eta\to3\pi$ Dalitz plot and the conversion photon spectrum without the measured matrix elements; a weight per simulated row corrects this. & Direct decay: Kroll--Wada $\times$ pion P-wave $\times|F_V|^2\times|F_{\mathrm{VMD}}|^2\times$ decay angles, divided by the generator density (mean weight 1, BR unchanged); bump: KLOE-2 Dalitz-plot parameters; conversion: $|F_V|^2$ photon spectrum; background rules. & yes (sim) & S0: $T_{12}$ in B $\times0.971$, in P $\times1.020$; C2+C3 in B $\times0.937$; C4 in P $\times1.063$; C5 in P $\times1.038$ \\
Beam energy & Data analysed at $T=4530$\,MeV, simulation generated at 4500\,MeV. & \MMfour and $y_4$ are recomputed for every simulated row at 4500\,MeV and for the data at 4530\,MeV; \Mfour, \Mpipi, \Mee, OA do not depend on it. SMASH cross sections are those at 4.5\,GeV. & yes (\MMfour, $y_4$) & \MMfour and $y_4$ spectra differ by the beam energy \\
Pions in the vertex fit (S0) & S0 keeps only rows whose two pion tracks were used in the event-vertex fit; the simulation must reproduce how often this happens. & S0/``pre'' of the $\eta$ peak and of the ECAL-tagged $\eta$ in data against simulation (WP8): peak $0.941\pm0.052$ vs 0.940, tagged $0.933\pm0.013$ vs 0.933. Not applied; it cancels in ratios. & no (S12, $\pm2.5$\,\%) & data/sim $1.002\pm0.056$ (peak), $1.000\pm0.014$ (tagged) \\
Corrected momenta (\code{cor_ok}) & The energy-loss corrected \pim momentum is missing for \pim at $\theta<16^\circ$ (0.62\,\% of the \ee rows). & Rows with \code{cor_ok} $=0$ are dropped in the data; the simulation has none. Fall-back (data only, test): the reconstructed \pim momentum. & yes & fall-back/nominal: B 1.014, P 1.013; on \Ypeak $+2.2\pm1.0$\,\% (one-sided, not applied) \\
Ghost tracks (variant (a)) & PAT marks some simulated lepton tracks as ghosts. A true one-source \ee pair with such a track counts as signal ($T_1$) in an $\eta$ channel but as a fake lepton ($T_3$) when it is background; the like-sign data do not contain these pairs, the data $N-\mathrm{CB}$ does. & Variant (a) moves the ghost-flagged true pairs from $T_3$ into $T_2$, so that signal and background are treated alike. Systematic (S4); the ruling is pending (D6). & variant & simulation $T_{12}$(a)$/T_{12}$: B 1.092, P 1.116, all \Mfour 1.162; the data/sim ratios fall by 8.5--14.5\,\% \\

Variants: mode B, $w_{\mathrm{db}}$. & yes (sim) & $T_{12}$ all \Mfour: mode B $\times1.055$, $w_{\mathrm{db}}$ $\times1.024$ \\
