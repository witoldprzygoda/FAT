Combinatorial background (CB) & Pairs of an $e^+$ and an $e^-$ that do not come from the same decay are removed by subtracting the like-sign pairs. & CB = 2 $\sqrt{N_{++}N_{--}}$ from the $e^+e^+$ and $e^-e^-$ rows of the DATA in 10 MeV M4 cells (k = 1), linearly interpolated to 2.5 MeV and spread over the cells of a layout by the like-sign share of the 50 MeV band; error from 200 Poisson event-group replicas. The simulation never provides it. & yes (data) & CB/N: S0 B 0.056, P 0.059; pre B 0.101, P 0.103 \\
Luminosity and PT3 word (64/63) & Turns counted rows into cross sections. & sigma = (N - CB) $\times$ 64/63 / L with L = 6079.2 nb$^{-1}$, the elastic-scattering luminosity summed over the 17 557 runs present in the data (START-free chain, LH2 only; sector-2 deficit removed inside L). 64/63: 1/64 of the PT3 events carry the trigger word 4096 and FAT keeps 8192. & yes & x 1.01587 / 6079.2 nb$^{-1}$ ($\pm$6.9 \%); old tables (6080.5, no 64/63) $\times$ 1.0161 \\

eta decay physics weights V3 (code 108) & The event generator decays eta $\to$ $\pi^+\pi^-e^+e^-$, the eta $\to$ 3pi Dalitz plot and the conversion photon spectrum without the measured matrix elements; each simulated row gets a weight that corrects this. & Direct decay: Kroll-Wada $\times$ pion P-wave $\times$ $|F_V|^2$ (pion form factor) $\times$ $|F_\mathrm{VMD}|^2$ $\times$ decay angles, divided by the generator density (mean weight 1, BR unchanged); bump: KLOE-2 Dalitz-plot parameters; conversion: $|F_V|^2$ photon spectrum; background rules. & yes (sim) & S0: T12 in B x0.9709, in P x1.0198; C2+C3 in B x0.937; C4 in P x1.063; C5 in P x1.038 \\
Beam energy & The data were analysed at T = 4530 MeV, the simulation was generated at 4500 MeV. & Variables that depend on the beam (missing mass MM4, rapidity y4) are recomputed for every simulated row at 4500 MeV and for the data at 4530 MeV; M4, M($\pi^+\pi^-$), M(ee), OA do not depend on it. The SMASH cross sections are those at 4.5 GeV and are not rescaled. & yes (MM4, y4) & MM4 and y4 spectra of data and simulation differ by the beam energy (Fig. B4 MM4, y4) \\

Corrected momenta (cor\_ok) & The energy-loss corrected $\pi^-$ momentum is missing (NaN) for $\pi^-$ at $\theta$ < 16 deg (0.62 \% of the $e^+e^-$ rows). & Rows with cor\_ok = 0 are dropped in data and simulation alike. Ghost tracks (variant (a)) & PAT marks some simulated lepton tracks as ghosts. A true one-source $e^+e^-$ pair with such a track counts as signal (T1) when its pions fit an eta channel, but as fake lepton (T3) when it is background; the like-sign data do not contain these pairs, so the data N - CB does. & Variant (a) moves the ghost-flagged true pairs from T3 into T2 and its groups, so that signal and background are treated alike. Shown dashed in Figs. B1-B4 and as column (a); a systematic (S4); the ruling is pending (D6). & variant & S0 sim T12(a)/T12: B 1.092, P 1.116, all M4 1.162 \\
