## 0. Headline statements (corrected after the review)
1. eta PEAK: the data peak is not larger than the simulated one. By the data-study method (P_main on data and simulation) it is 0.36-0.84
   of the simulation; methods that use the simulated background shape (template fit, side bands) give 0.66-1.36. The peak normalisation is
   known only to a factor of about 2 (section 6). WITHDRAWN: 'about 3.3 times larger' (method A, all-M4 content: 0.29-0.41) and 'about 2 times'.
2. The simulated direct decay eta -> pi+pi-e+e- has a non-QED generator M(ee) (largest at threshold, close to 1/M^2): its yield, the
   direct/conversion ratio, the class shapes and the OA efficiencies are not for physics. To report to the producers (PLUTO database_decays_eta.dat).
3. The QED-reshaped direct decay is an ILLUSTRATION of shapes only (flat acceptance and fixed content assumed); no fit result of it is quoted.
4. Qualitatively the data peak has far more content at OA > 10 deg and M(ee) > 20 MeV than either simulated component (section 4; data errors
   now include the fit-model spread, so the chi2 values are smaller than before).
5. eta BUMP: the simulated bump is too large relative to the simulated background at the bump edge; the fit factor lies between about 0.13
   and 0.51 depending on model, range, mass scale and ghost variant (poor fits, not a yield). The eta-related background gets 0.55-2.29 (mostly > 1)
   in the same fits, which contradicts a bump made too large by the same etas: never combine bump and peak factors into an eta production factor.
6. Above the eta structures the spectrum is dominated by multi-pion background (sim share E1/E2: 81/81 % in 0.56-0.70, 88/90 % in 0.70-1.00), which
   SMASH does not describe. Its level there is 0.87-1.36 times SMASH (E1, E2; T1+T2 or variant (a); vz > -160 or the central slice
   -140 < vz < -100 mm), with L_data unconfirmed. No selection reaches eta dominance outside the bump window (sim purity E1/E2: bump window
