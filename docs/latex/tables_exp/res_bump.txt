WHY NO MODEL-FREE BUMP YIELD: the bump (eta -> pi+pi-pi0 with an e+e- pair from the pi0 and the photon lost) has no shape of its own in M4:
it is a broad distribution from about 0.40 up to the kinematic edge (0.49-0.52), lying on a multi-pion background whose shape under the
bump is not known (no side band below the bump; above the edge lies the eta peak; at the edge the drop h is only 0.2-0.5 of the level of
N - CB). A smooth polynomial absorbs most of the bump,
so N - CB - background in the bump window depends entirely on the background model. Only the sharp drop at the predicted edge is
model-independent: h measures the bump density just below the edge, per M(pipi) slice. Turning E (or W) into a number of eta -> pi+pi-pi0
decays needs the bump shape below the edge (Dalitz-plot matrix element, pi0 Dalitz / conversion mix, acceptance): this comes from the
simulation and is done in the next report. The ECAL-tagged eta T is a clean peak but counts only the events with a detected photon.
